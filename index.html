About

Each line is a path of the Collatz map from a random starting value down to 1, drawn outward from 1 with a left turn at each even number and a right turn at each odd number. Edges shared by more paths are drawn thicker and darker. New paths are added continuously, and the oldest are removed.

The style follows the figure by Dmitry Kobak on Wikipedia.

Source code: github.com/ChristopherMayes/collatz-tracker
